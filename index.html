03 — Why thermal process

Purity is decided at the acid, not at the filter.

Commodity route · wet process
a.

Phosphate rock + sulfuric acid

The acid carries Fe, Al, Mg, F and heavy metals out of the ore.

b.

Purification, if any

Each impurity removed costs money — fertilizer-grade acid mostly skips it.

c.

MKP with residue risk

Insolubles ride along — into the tank, the dripper, the complaint.

Our route · thermal process
a.

Yellow phosphorus, burned

Elemental P is refined before the acid exists — ore-bound impurities do not carry through the same path.

b.

Hydration to thermal acid

Structurally clean phosphoric acid from our own heat-recovery line.

c.

Neutralized to salts

Low insolubles, low heavy metals — by design, verified on every lot.
