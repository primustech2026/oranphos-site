Methods

How the numbers are made.

In-house, every lot

Assay by titration, insolubles by gravimetric method, chloride and pH per industry methods. No lot is released without a signed COA.

Third party, on request

SGS sampling at loading, independent assay, and load supervision on every trial container — at our arrangement.

Retained samples

A sealed sample of every export lot is kept on file, so any later question has a physical answer.
