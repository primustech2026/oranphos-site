Common questions

The answers your first email would ask for.

What is the minimum order?
One 20' trial container — 25 mt, 1,000 × 25 kg bags. After qualification, most partners settle into 1–2 containers per month.
What are the payment terms?
30% T/T deposit, 70% against B/L copy for trial containers. Terms are reviewed after a stable ordering history.
Can you produce against our specification?
Send your target spec. We confirm line by line against recent lot data — and tell you plainly if a parameter is outside what we will commit to.
How do we verify quality before ordering?
The 10-lot COA record up front, a free DHL sample for your own lab, and SGS sampling plus loading supervision on the trial container.
Do you support private label?
Yes — from the first container. Send artwork; we handle bag printing with pre-production proofs.
Which ports and terms do you quote?
FOB Shanghai or Qingdao as standard; CIF on request. Every quote states its validity window — MKP pricing moves, and we re-confirm before contract.
How fast can a sample arrive?
We ship 1–2 kg by DHL the day after your request — typically 5–7 days door to door, with the matching lot COA in the box and by email.
Can you ship less than a full container (LCL)?
Yes, for first evaluations. LCL costs more per ton and adds handling — most partners move to FCL from the second order. We quote both so you can compare.
Which documents come with a shipment?
Commercial invoice, packing list, COA, MSDS, certificate of origin, and B/L as standard. Additional documents (phytosanitary-style declarations, specific certificates) — tell us your import requirements and we confirm before contract.
How is HS classification and import duty handled?
We export under HS 2835.24 (potassium phosphates, chemical chapter). Import-side classification and duty are set by your customs authority — we provide full documentation to support your broker either way.
What is the shelf life and how should it be stored?
Five years in original sealed bags, stored dry and off the floor. MKP is not classified as dangerous goods — it ships and stores as general cargo.
Does MKP cake during shipping?
Free-flowing crystals with low moisture (≤0.3%) plus PE-lined bags keep caking risk low. Keep pallets out of rain during unloading and reseal opened bags.
What happens if a lot fails our incoming inspection?
Send your lab result with the bag lot number. We test the retained sample of that exact lot within 48 hours and share raw data. If the lot is out of spec, replacement or refund per contract — that is what retained samples are for.
Can we audit or visit the plant?
Yes. Plant visits are welcome by appointment, and we support remote video audits — line walk-through, lab, warehouse — if travel is not practical.
Do you offer regional exclusivity?
Not at trial stage. After a stable ordering history we discuss protection for your market on volume commitments — put it on the table when you are ready.
What is the minimum for private label printing?
One container (1,000 bags). Send artwork, we return a pre-production proof for sign-off before printing.
Can MKP and DKP ship in one container?
Yes — mixed containers across our phosphate range are routine, palletized and marked by product. Useful when you want to qualify two products with one shipment.
How is my first payment protected?
30/70 T/T against B/L copy means the balance moves only after goods are on the water with documents in hand. Add SGS loading supervision and you have third-party eyes on quantity and quality before you pay the balance.
