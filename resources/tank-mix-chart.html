MKP tank-mix compatibility

One rule belongs above every dosing skid: MKP lives in B; calcium lives in A.

Material with MKPConcentrated stock statusOperating note
UreaGenerally compatibleConfirm final concentration, temperature and crop EC.
Potassium sulfateGenerally compatibleSolubility can limit dense stock, especially under cold conditions.
Magnesium sulfateCompatible with cautionRun a jar test at actual concentration, pH and minimum-night temperature.
Chelated micronutrients · EDTAUsually compatible in the chelate pH windowFollow label limits and check the full blend after 2 and 12 hours.
Calcium nitrate or any calcium sourceABSOLUTELY PROHIBITEDCalcium + phosphate forms poorly soluble calcium phosphate precipitate.
Non-chelated iron sulfateUse cautionHard water and high-pH solutions can destabilize iron and create deposits.
Permanent tank rule: MKP and other phosphates stay in B. Calcium nitrate and other calcium sources stay in A. Use separate transfer hoses and non-return valves.

Jar test for concentrated stock

  1. Use 1 L actual source water.
  2. Scale to the intended stock concentration.
  3. Add materials in the planned order.
  4. Inspect at 5 minutes, 2 hours and 12 hours.
  5. Reject haze, floc, crystals, sediment or heat.

A clear diluted line does not authorize a shared A/B concentrate. Dilution widens the compatibility window only after separate injection.
