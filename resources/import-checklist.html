Phosphate import document checklist

Use this sheet for a 25 t MKP, DKP, urea phosphate or sodium-phosphate shipment before the vessel cut-off.

Core commercial set

Documents expected on every shipment.

Pro forma invoice

Product, grade, quantity, 25 kg packing, Incoterm, port, price, validity, 30/70 T/T terms and production lead time.

Commercial invoice + packing list

Matching consignee, product description, bag count, net/gross weight, pallet count and invoice references.

Certificate of Analysis

COA lot number must match the lot printed on the bags and listed in the packing records.

MSDS / SDS

Current GHS-format safety data sheet with correct product identity, hazards, handling and transport status.

Certificate of origin

Issued or endorsed in the form required by the destination country and trade arrangement.

Bill of lading

Check shipper, consignee, notify party, ports, container and seal number, package count, weights and product wording before release.

Optional qualification set

Add what your market or customer requires.

SGS inspection certificate

Sampling, assay, bag count, container condition and loading supervision for the first trial container.

ISO certificates

ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 copies, with certificate holder and validity checked.

Halal / Kosher certificates

MUI Indonesia, Malaysia Halal or Star-K Kosher documents when required by the target customer.

HS classification statement

Support for HS 2835.24 or the destination broker’s confirmed classification and local tariff line.

Red flags before payment or release

  • COA has no lot number or the lot does not match the bag.
  • A specification sheet is offered in place of a batch COA.
  • The seller refuses SGS sampling or loading supervision on the trial shipment.
