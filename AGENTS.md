# ORANPHOS Content Rules

These rules apply to all English knowledge-base articles under `resources/`.

- Voice: an experienced export engineer speaking directly to a buyer. Be specific, practical, and restrained.
- Do not use: `comprehensive`, `robust`, `leverage`, `cutting-edge`, `game-changer`, `revolutionize`, `delve into`, `In today's`, `In the world of`, or `When it comes to`.
- Every paragraph must include at least one specific number or material name. Keep paragraphs to 3–4 sentences and never more than 6 sentences.
- Do not open with a definition. Open with the specified concrete operating scene.
- The first `h2` must be the target keyword written as a question. Follow it immediately with a 40–60 word direct answer suitable for a featured snippet.
- Use HTML `<table>` elements for comparisons and `<ol>` elements for procedures.
- End each article with:
  - `Key Takeaways` followed by a `<ul>` of 3–5 items.
  - The byline `ORANPHOS Technical Team`.
  - The disclaimer `Typical ranges from published references — verify with your agronomist`.
  - A CTA linking to `mailto:sales@oranphos.com`.
- Article `h1` and `h2` headings must not contain `MKP supplier` or `MKP manufacturer`.
- Each article must contain exactly one natural-context link to `../products-mkp.html`.
- Target length: 800–1,000 English words per article.
