The company

ORANPHOS is the working brand for a phosphate manufacturer in Fengxiang, Baoji, Shaanxi. The 82-mu site combines a 20,000 t/y thermal phosphoric acid line with four phosphate-salt lines rated at 50,000 t/y.

Production covers 6 series and more than 20 products, including MKP, DKP, urea phosphate, food-, industrial- and electronic-grade phosphoric acid, TSP and DSP. Export buyers deal with the same plant team for lot COAs, 25 kg packing, SGS loading supervision and container documents.

The full record

Five decisions that built the current plant.

2009

Baoji Fenghui commissioned its first phosphate production unit in 2009, at a time when Northwest China had no other dedicated producer combining phosphate R&D with regular commercial output. The starting operation was smaller than the present 50,000 t/y salt system, but it established the core disciplines that still matter: raw-material qualification, neutralization control, crystallization, filtration and batch release. Local fertilizer and industrial customers provided the first operating feedback, while the technical team built formulations around MKP, DKP and sodium phosphates. The company’s location in Baoji also created a practical base for serving western agricultural markets without relying on an eastern-coast trading warehouse. This period formed the predecessor business and the production knowledge later transferred into ORANPHOS.

2015

By 2015, repeat industrial demand had outgrown the original workshop configuration, so the company doubled phosphate-salt capacity and tightened control of the batch sequence. Expansion was not limited to adding vessels: utilities, crystallizers, filtration, drying and bagging had to move at the same rate or the nominal increase would have created a bottleneck. The plant standardized incoming checks for potassium and sodium alkalis, then aligned in-process samples with final COA release. Higher throughput also justified clearer separation between fertilizer, food-related and industrial production schedules. The result was a more repeatable operating window for products such as MKP and DKP, and enough stable volume to support buyers beyond the immediate Shaanxi market. That capacity step prepared the factory for export qualification two years later.

2017

The first export shipment left in 2017, ending the plant’s zero-export period and changing how each lot had to be documented. Overseas buyers required English COAs, GHS-format SDS files, consistent 25 kg PE-lined bags, pallet plans and a traceable link between the batch number and shipping documents. The team also had to work backward from vessel cut-off dates, not just forward from production completion. A single 25 t container exposed practical details that domestic dispatch could absorb more easily: pallet dimensions, container moisture risk, label wording, customs classification and B/L review. Those first shipments established HS 2835.24 routines and direct communication between QC, export sales and logistics. The export book remained modest, but the operating system had crossed the important threshold from factory-ready to border-ready.

2019

In 2019, the business was reorganized around a RMB 300 million investment and the present 82-mu integrated fine-phosphate campus. The new plan allocated 24,500 m² to production workshops and 5,000 m² to R&D and administration, creating room for 4 salt lines rather than incremental extensions to the old layout. Investment also brought production, laboratory, warehousing and export packing into one controlled site. This mattered commercially because an MKP buyer could now trace the same lot from neutralization through crystal handling, 25 kg bagging and container loading. The company expanded its development scope to 6 product series and more than 20 products while building a patent portfolio that now totals 15 items. The reorganization is the point at which the current manufacturing platform took shape.

2021

The 20,000 t/y thermal phosphoric acid line entered operation in 2021, giving the plant direct control over the acid that determines phosphate purity. Yellow phosphorus combustion and hydration were placed under DCS and SIS control, while heat recovery captured energy that would otherwise leave with the process gas. Owning the acid line reduced dependence on commodity wet-process acid and let the QC team manage chloride, metals and insoluble risk before neutralization begins. The same campus could now produce food-, industrial- and electronic-grade acid alongside the feedstock for MKP, DKP and urea phosphate. A waste-acid recycling route further tightened material use. For export buyers, the practical change was simple: the plant could explain the origin of the acid, provide batch evidence and answer a purity question without passing it to an upstream supplier.
